The hour's movers

Each mover compares its newest reading with a reading about an hour older, and prints the real gap on its row. A trend with no older reading is not a mover, however hot it looks.

What the statuses mean

Working means the source answered on the latest check and found something. Found nothing means it answered and came back empty, which happens: one of our two Reddit sources is blocked so often that its twin carries Reddit instead. Out of date means the latest check skipped the source, so its finds print with their real date and stay out of the totals. Off on purpose is TikTok and X: there is no honest way to read them with what we have, so they claim nothing and their data is never faked. Retired means closed for good, with the reason on its row.

Every number on this page traces to a reading we stored. The movers come from those same stored readings, and trends that did not move are counted in the banner, not hidden.
