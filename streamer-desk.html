🎙 The Streamer Desk

Who streams where, what they're up to, which houses they run with — and a drama tracker with a hard rule: a timeline prints only what two or more independent sources verify. Anything weaker sits in quarantine, badged unverified, with the reason on the record. Rosters are dated claims, not eternal facts. No invented numbers, ever.

The directory

Major streamers: platforms, main games and categories, crews and houses. Every profile carries its sources; facts are dated as of the curation stamp.

What they're up to

Dated entries only: records, platform moves, events, collabs. Newest first.

Drama tracker — verified timelines

Timeline format, neutral tone, every event sourced and timestamped. "Verified" means two or more independent credible sources report the event. Allegations are reported as allegations, with who made them.

Quarantine — unverified

Claims in circulation that do not meet the bar. They are printed here — labeled, with the reason — so nobody mistakes their absence from the timelines for ignorance, or their presence here for fact.

Streamer houses

The crews and orgs: membership as of a stated date, what they produce, and who has left. A house that closed stays listed — as closed.

Events calendar

Conventions, awards, charity marathons, subathons. Past and upcoming are computed from the dates at build time — a past event never poses as upcoming.

Desk feeds — the honest lanes

The Desk's one live lane runs on the Live Wire (the streamer-beat news lane, real recorded hits only). The directory APIs below cannot be fed honestly with the credentials this project holds, so they ship skip-by-design — no live status, no follower counts, nothing faked.

How this desk works

The payload (data/v1/streamer-desk.json) is built by scripts/build_streamer_desk.js from a hand-curated record (data/streamer_desk.json). The generator enforces the sourcing standard in code: a profile or house with no source, a drama timeline whose events trace to fewer than two distinct source domains, an undated roster, or an unverified item with no stated reason makes the build fail — the Desk would rather not publish than launder a claim. Directory facts need one credible source; drama needs two.
