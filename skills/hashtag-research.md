# Research hashtags for a post

## Use when

Publishing anything riding a trend.

## Inputs

- `data/v1/hashtags.json` (tag → topic index)
- The topic dossier's hashtags

## Procedure

1. Take the dossier's hashtags as your core 2–3 (they're the ones with momentum).
2. Add 1 broad tag for the category (#gaming, #spookyseason) and 1 niche tag for your community.
3. Never exceed 5 — more tags dilute, they don't multiply.
4. Check `hashtags.json`: if a tag maps to 3+ topics, it's a live lane; if it maps to none, it's dead — drop it.
5. Platform rules: TikTok/IG favor in-caption tags; YouTube favors 2–3 in description + title keywords over tags.

## Worked example

Posting a Ramalama walk: #halloweencostume (peaking) + #ramalama (format tag) + #spookyseason (broad seasonal) + your niche tag. Four tags, all live.

## Pitfalls

- #fyp #viral #foryou — dead weight; the algorithm ignores them.
- Copying 30 hashtags from a "viral hashtags" listicle — half are stale.
- Using a peaking tag after the moment passed — check the dossier's freshness first.
