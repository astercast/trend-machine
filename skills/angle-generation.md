# Generate content angles from a trend

## Use when

You have a hot topic and need video ideas in the next 10 minutes.

## Inputs

- A topic dossier (title, summary, category, platforms)
- Your niche, format, and voice

## Procedure

1. List the topic's core tension in one sentence (e.g. "everyone's doing the dance badly on purpose").
2. Generate one angle per format you run: reaction, explainer, parody, ranking, tutorial.
3. Score each angle: speed to make (hours) × fit to your voice (1–5) × novelty (has your niche done it yet?).
4. Kill any angle scoring under 12. Ship the top 2.
5. Write the hook first (see dossier hooks for patterns), then the video.

## Worked example

Topic: "Ramalama Costume Walk" (Halloween format, withheld-reveal hook).
Angles for a couples channel: (1) synchronized stalk walk in couples costumes;
(2) expectation-vs-reality: mundane costume, maximum menace; (3) tutorial: how to nail the low-angle shot.
Angle 2 wins on novelty — nobody's parodied the menace yet.

## Pitfalls

- Copying the dossier angles verbatim — they're starting points; your audience follows YOU.
- More than 2 angles per topic — you split your own momentum.
- Angles that need a week of production on a 3-day trend — match production time to trend half-life.
