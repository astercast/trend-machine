# Remix a viral format

## Use when

A meme/format is hot and you want in without cloning.

## Inputs

- The format's mechanics (hook, structure, payoff) from its dossier
- Your niche's native material

## Procedure

1. Strip the format to its skeleton: hook type (withheld reveal? contrast? objection?), beat structure, payoff.
2. Keep the skeleton, swap the skin: your niche's characters, stakes, and jokes.
3. Change ONE structural element (the POV, the timing, the payoff target) — that's what makes it yours and what the algorithm rewards as novel.
4. Test the first 2 seconds on a friend: if they get the format instantly AND laugh at your twist, ship it.
5. Credit the format in a comment or caption when it's a direct lift — the community notices and rewards it.

## Worked example

Format: "Two Fishes" deadpan defense. Skeleton: unreasonable take + TED Talk patience + lip-sync.
Remix for a finance channel: "Having two credit cards doesn't mean you have to max one out" — deadpan, straight to camera. Same skeleton, finance skin, one twist (it's actually good advice).

## Pitfalls

- Skin-swap with no twist — the algorithm has seen it 10,000 times.
- Breaking the skeleton — if you change the hook AND the structure, it's not a remix, it's a new video wearing a costume.
- Remixing a format your audience doesn't know — the joke needs the reference to land.
