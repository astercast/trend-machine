05How the machine works

1 · Fetch

Twelve fetchers sweep Google Trends, news, Reddit, Hacker News, Mastodon, Bluesky, YouTube, TikTok, and X every 15 minutes.

2 · Normalize

Hits are deduped, merged, and scored: velocity from corroboration + recency, opportunity from velocity ÷ saturation.

3 · Publish

Dashboards, topic dossiers, and the versioned JSON API rebuild. Agents read data/v1/trends.json directly.

✎ curator's note

Every other trend tool is a trophy case — here's what already won. By the time a topic makes a leaderboard, the window for making something about it is closing. That's why the Oracle asks the opposite question: what's still climbing?

The trajectory map is the whole bet. And when a forecast is wrong, the scorecard says so in public. I'd rather be interesting and wrong on the record than safe and vague.
