How it works

  1. 1

    Watch

    Watchers pull Google Trends, news, Reddit, Hacker News, Mastodon, Bluesky, YouTube, TikTok and X into one record.

  2. 2

    Score

    Repeats merge into one trend each. Every trend gets a spreading score, and the board score: spreading discounted by how crowded the lane already is.

  3. 3

    Publish

    The board, every trend's page, and the public data feed rebuild from those scores. Every page prints the snapshot it came from.
