Topic Platforms

Opportunity = velocity × (1 − saturation penalty). Stale topics (>72h without corroboration) are dimmed and flagged — treat them as cooling. Every topic links to a dossier with content angles, hooks, and hashtags. Machine-readable feed: api/v1/trends.json.

How the machine works

1 · Fetch

Pluggable fetchers hit Google Trends, Google News, Reddit, YouTube, and X (BYO key) every 15 minutes.

2 · Normalize

Hits are deduped, merged, and scored: velocity from corroboration + recency, opportunity from velocity ÷ saturation.

3 · Publish

Dashboards, topic dossiers, and the versioned JSON API rebuild. Agents read api/v1/trends.json directly.
