05How the machine works

1 · Fetch

Twelve fetchers sweep Google Trends, news, Reddit, Hacker News, Mastodon, Bluesky, YouTube, TikTok, and X every 15 minutes.

2 · Normalize

Hits are deduped, merged, and scored: velocity from corroboration + recency, opportunity from velocity ÷ saturation.

3 · Publish

Dashboards, topic dossiers, and the versioned JSON API rebuild. Agents read data/v1/trends.json directly.
