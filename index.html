05How it works

  1. 1

    Watch

    Twelve watchers check Google Trends, news, Reddit, Hacker News, Mastodon, Bluesky, YouTube, TikTok, and X every 15 minutes.

  2. 2

    Score

    Repeats merge into one trend each. Every trend gets a spreading score, and the board score: spreading discounted by how crowded the lane already is.

  3. 3

    Publish

    The board, every trend's page, and the public data feed rebuild from those scores.

✎ curator's note

Every other trend tool is a trophy case, here's what already won. By the time a topic makes a leaderboard, the window for making something about it is closing. That's why the board asks the opposite question: what's climbing right now?

The board is the whole bet: what's hot at this moment, with the evidence on every row and the lane record underneath it. I'd rather be interesting and wrong on the record than safe and vague.
