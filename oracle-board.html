How to read this board

A row is born when the Oracle makes a decisive call — peaking, accelerating, rising, or cooling off — and the ledger records it with the velocity and the snapshot count behind the call. The trajectory is drawn from the topic's stored snapshots only; a series under 3 points says "history building" instead of drawing a line that isn't there.

The window column counts the actual snapshots that have landed inside the row's own 48 hours. Two or more and the window is ready — the graders will have evidence to judge. Fewer and it is short: if it is still short at its horizon it grades unverifiable, counted apart and never folded into the hit rate. Verdicts are written by the graders in scripts/resolution-lib.js against the classifier's own thresholds — the same rules the scorecard publishes.
